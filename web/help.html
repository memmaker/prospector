Keys to remember

Enter all commands · # explore · < > walk to stairs, ship or station · E items · ? the game's help menu (manual, keybindings, configuration) · S save and quit · numpad or arrows: move.

Saved games stay in this browser (IndexedDB); Export save downloads them. When a game ends, press a key for a new one.
